Rajma Recipe

Classic Rajma Masala: A Hearty North Indian Favorite

Ingredients

  • 1 cup kidney beans (rajma), soaked overnight
  • 3 tablespoons oil or ghee
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 small cinnamon stick
  • 2 cloves
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds (jeera)
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped
  • 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 tomatoes, pureed
  • 1 teaspoon red chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon coriander powder
  • ½ teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • Salt, as needed
  • 1 cup water (adjust for consistency)
  • Fresh cilantro, chopped (for garnish)

Steps

  1. Cook Kidney Beans:
    • Soak kidney beans overnight and pressure cook them with 3 cups of water and a pinch of salt until soft. Set aside along with the cooking liquid.
  2. Prepare the Gravy:
    • Heat oil or ghee in a pan. Add bay leaf, cinnamon stick, cloves, and cumin seeds. Sauté until aromatic.
    • Add finely chopped onions and sauté until golden brown.
    • Stir in ginger-garlic paste and cook for a minute.
    • Add cut tomato or puree and cook until the mixture thickens and the oil separates.
  3. Spices and Simmering:
    • Add red chili powder, coriander powder, turmeric powder, garam masala, and salt. Mix well and cook for a few minutes.
    • Pour in a cup of water and simmer the gravy for 5–7 minutes.
  4. Combine Beans and Gravy:
    • Add the cooked kidney beans along with their cooking liquid. Stir well and simmer for 10–15 minutes to allow the flavors to meld.
  5. Finish:
    • Garnish with freshly chopped cilantro.

Serve this hearty Rajma with steamed rice or roti for a comforting meal.
