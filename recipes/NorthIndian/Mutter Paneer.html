🌿 Mutter Paneer

Mutter paneer is a rich, creamy, and spiced North Indian curry made with soft paneer cheese cubes and green peas in a savory tomato-onion sauce

Ingredients

  • Paneer – 250 grams, cubed
  • Green peas – 1 cup (boiled or frozen)
  • Onion – 1 medium, chopped
  • Tomatoes – 2, pureed
  • Green chillies – 2
  • Garlic cloves – 2
  • Ginger – 1 inch piece
  • Cashew nuts – 5
  • Poppy seeds – 1 tablespoon
  • Cumin seeds – 1 teaspoon
  • Kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves) – 1 teaspoon
  • Red chilli powder – 1 teaspoon
  • Turmeric Powder – 1/2 teaspoon
  • Everest Kitchen King Masala – 2 teaspoons
  • Oil – 1 tablespoon
  • Ghee – 3 tablespoons
  • Salt – to taste

Instructions

  1. Prepare the onion paste: Heat 1 tablespoon of oil in a pan. Fry the chopped onion, green chillies, garlic, ginger, cashews, poppy seeds, cumin until soft. Soak this mixture in water for 30 minutes, then grind it into a smooth paste.
  2. Sauté the paste: Heat the ghee in a pan. Add the ground onion paste and fry it on medium heat for about 3 minutes until fragrant.
  3. Add tomatoes and spices: Stir in the pureed tomatoes, red chilli powder, Everest Turmeric Powder, and Everest Kitchen King Masala. Add the boiled green peas and salt.
  4. Simmer the gravy: Cook the mixture until the ghee separates from the masala.
  5. Add paneer: Pour in water as needed for gravy consistency, then gently add the paneer cubes and kasuri methi Cover and cook on low heat for 5 minutes before serving hot.

Notes

  • Adjust the gravy thickness by adding more or less water.
  • For a richer taste, you can add a spoonful of fresh cream at the end.
  • Serve hot with roti, naan, or steamed rice.
