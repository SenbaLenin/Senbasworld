🥬 Palak Paneer

Simple and delicious homemade spinach and paneer curry

Ingredients

  • 250 g spinach (palak)
  • 1 green chili
  • 1 medium onion
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 teaspoon cumin (jeera)
  • 1 medium tomato
  • 1 teaspoon coriander powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt, or to taste
  • 1/2 teaspoon garam masala
  • 1 tspoon Kasuri methi
  • 200 g paneer
  • 1–2 tablespoons oil

Instructions

  1. Add spinach, green chili, onion, garlic, cumin, tomato, coriander powder, and turmeric powder to a pan.
  2. Add a little water, cover, and cook until the spinach and vegetables become soft.
  3. Turn off the heat and blend the cooked mixture with a hand blender until smooth.
  4. Add salt to taste and mix well.
  5. Cut the paneer into cubes and lightly fry them in oil until lightly golden.
  6. Add the fried paneer pieces to the spinach gravy.
  7. Add garam masala and Kasuri methi mix well.(optional)
  8. Simmer for a few minutes so the paneer absorbs the flavors.

Notes

  • Don’t overcook the spinach to keep its fresh green color and flavor.
  • Simmer the paneer gently so it stays soft and absorbs the gravy well.
  • Serve hot with roti, naan, or steamed rice.
